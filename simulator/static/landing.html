AI Finance Controller

Payment-to-invoice reconciliation that knows when it doesn't know.

Razorpay Buildathon · Track 04

The problem

A merchant sends out hundreds of bills a month. Money comes back into their bank account. But the bank doesn't say which bill each payment settles — it just says money arrived.

So every morning someone in finance sits with two lists and connects them by hand. This is called reconciliation.

Razorpay's virtual accounts already solved half of it: each customer pays into their own account number, so you always know WHO paid. Nobody solved WHICH BILL it settles.

The solution

L0Who sent this money?
L1Did they write the bill number, and does the amount agree?
L2Is the shortage small enough to ignore?
L3Can AI make sense of messy text?
L4Does a house rule settle it?
L5We could not prove it. Here is why.
L6Here is what we will do about it.
Rules where proof exists. AI where interpretation is needed. Refusal where neither works.
